Vendredi 0.1.3 adds an AppImage for Linux. The simulator itself is unchanged from 0.1.2.

### Linux AppImage

- Download the AppImage for your architecture, mark it executable, and open it. Vendredi appears with its icon and uses your workspace in `~/Vendredi`, creating the sample workspace on first launch.
- The AppImage is also the command-line tool. Arguments such as `run --headless WORKSPACE` work as they do with the plain executable.
- It uses your desktop's X11 or Wayland and OpenGL libraries, and needs FUSE to start directly. Without FUSE, run it with `--appimage-extract-and-run`.
- The `.tar.gz` downloads still contain the single executable, for servers and scripts.

Both Linux builds are made on Ubuntu 24.04 and need a distribution with glibc 2.39 or newer.
